See where your money goes.

Sortd is an iPhone app that keeps a list of what you spend and sorts it into categories.

What it does

  • Logs Apple Pay taps. You set up a short automation in Apple's Shortcuts app once. After that, each in-store Apple Pay tap is added to Sortd, even when the app is closed.
  • Reads receipts from Gmail, if you want. Sortd can search your Gmail for receipts and bank alerts and add them as purchases. The emails are read on your iPhone. Gmail is optional.
  • Handles many currencies. Purchases in other currencies are converted to your home currency using that day's European Central Bank rate.
  • Spots repeat payments. Sortd notices payments that repeat, like subscriptions, and shows when the next one is due. It can remind you the day before.

Your data stays on your iPhone. There's no account to make and no Sortd server.
