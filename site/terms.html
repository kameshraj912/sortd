Check your bank statements

Sortd builds its list from Apple Pay payments, receipts you scan, statements you import, and what you enter. Apple's Wallet trigger can miss a payment, and cash and cards that aren't in Apple Pay are not seen at all. These can be missing, late, or wrong. When Sortd reads a receipt, a statement or a line you typed (with simple rules or with Apple Intelligence on your iPhone), it can misread it, so check what it fills in before you save. Converted amounts use published exchange rates, which may differ from what your bank charged. Always check your bank statement for the exact figures.
