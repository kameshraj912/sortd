How do I log Apple Pay taps automatically?

You set up one automation in Apple's Shortcuts app. It takes about a minute, and Sortd's setup shows a picture of every step. You can also find it later in Settings › Purchase Sources › Apple Pay Logging.

The quick way

  1. Get the ready-made shortcut on your iPhone, then tap the download, then Add Shortcut. It already knows which part of the tap is the amount, the shop and the card.
  2. In Shortcuts, open Automation and tap +.
  3. Choose Wallet, pick the cards you pay with, choose Run Immediately, and turn Notify When Run off.
  4. Add the action Run Shortcut, choose Log Apple Pay in Sortd, and set its input to Shortcut Input.
  5. Tap back to save. Pay with Apple Pay in a shop and it shows up in Sortd.

By hand

  1. Open Shortcuts and tap + at the bottom. On the next screen tap Edit at the top right.
  2. Tap the blue Automation chip under the search box, type wallet, then tap Wallet. The shortcut now starts with "When Any Card is tapped".
  3. In the search box at the bottom, type Sortd and tap Log Wallet Tap.
  4. Tap Amount, then Select Variable, then Shortcut Input. Now tap that blue word again and choose Amount. This second tap is the part people miss: without it, iOS says "couldn't convert from Transaction to Text" and nothing is logged.
  5. Do the same for Shop (choose Merchant). Tap › to show Card and choose Card or Pass.
  6. Tap back to save. That's it.

Pressing ▶ in Shortcuts is only a test: it has no purchase in it, so Sortd replies "connected" and saves nothing. Pay with Apple Pay in a shop to log a real one. Only in-store taps trigger the automation. Online and in-app purchases come in from email receipts if you connect Gmail.

A tap shows the wrong shop, amount or card

Open Settings › Purchase Sources › Apple Pay Logging in Sortd. Under Last Tap Received you'll see exactly what Apple Pay sent. If Apple Pay sent no amount, Sortd still saves the purchase and marks it so you can add the amount.

How do I connect Gmail?

Gmail is optional. In Sortd, go to Settings › Purchase Sources › Connect Gmail, read the short explanation, and tap "Continue with Google". Sign in and allow Sortd to read your email. Sortd can only read; it can't send, delete or change anything.

Sortd then searches for receipts and bank alerts from the last 120 days and adds them. After that it checks for new ones when you open the app, or when you tap Sync Now.

Google says the app isn't verified

While Sortd is being reviewed by Google, Google shows a warning that the app hasn't been verified. During this testing period:

  • Only Gmail accounts added as testers can connect.
  • To continue past the warning, tap "Advanced", then the link to go on to Sortd.
  • Google may end access after 7 days. If Sortd says Google access has ended, disconnect that Gmail and connect it again.

Why was a receipt missed?

  • No amount in the email. Sortd only adds a purchase when it can find the total in the email text. It never guesses an amount.
  • The receipt is only a link. Some emails just say "view your receipt" and link to a website. Sortd reads the email itself, not linked pages, so there's nothing to read.
  • It didn't look like a receipt. Sortd searches for emails Gmail files under Purchases, or with subjects like "receipt", "invoice", "order confirmation", "payment received" or "refund". Emails in Gmail's Promotions or Social tabs, and marketing subjects (sales, discounts, newsletters), are skipped.
  • It's too old. The first sync looks back 120 days.

You can always add a purchase by hand.

Which last 4 digits should I enter for a card?

Sortd matches purchases to your cards by their last 4 digits. A card can have two numbers:

  • Card number: the last 4 digits printed on your card or shown in your bank's app. Bank emails usually show this one.
  • Apple Pay number (Device Account Number): Apple Pay pays with its own number, so Apple Pay receipts and taps often show this instead. Find it in Wallet › your card › ••• › Card Details › Device Account Number. Your Apple Watch has a different one from your iPhone; Sortd usually learns it the first time you pay with the Watch.

If you have two cards from the same bank, Sortd asks for the Apple Pay number of each, because that's the only way to tell them apart when you tap. Sortd only ever asks for the last 4 digits, never the full number.

How do currencies work?

  • Purchase currency follows your iPhone's time zone, so spending while you travel is recorded in the local currency.
  • Totals are shown in your home currency. Change it in Settings › Currency › Show Totals In.
  • Purchases in other currencies are converted using that day's European Central Bank rate, downloaded from frankfurter.dev. On weekends and holidays the most recent earlier rate is used. Tap Update Exchange Rates if a conversion is missing.

Converted amounts are estimates. Your bank's rate and fees may differ.

How do I change my check-in or setup answers?

  • Check-in: Settings › Bills & Reminders › Check-in. Pick Each morning, Each evening, Sunday recap, or Only when it matters (no regular check-ins). It never shows amounts.
  • Setup answers: Settings › Help & Feedback › Run Setup Again. Your purchases and cards stay as they are.

If the check-in never arrives, notifications may be off for Sortd. Turn them on in your iPhone's Settings › Notifications › Sortd.

How do I delete my data?

  • Everything: Settings › Backup & Data › Delete All Data. This removes every purchase, card, budget, setting and setup answer from your iPhone, turns off your check-in and reminders, and disconnects Gmail. If iCloud backup is on, the iCloud copy goes too, and if you're signed in, you're signed out. It can't be undone.
  • One Gmail account: Settings › Purchase Sources, find the account under Email Receipts, then tap Disconnect. You can also delete the purchases only that account added.
  • A copy first: Settings › Backup & Data › Save a Backup, or Export as Spreadsheet.

Your purchases are only on your iPhone (and in your own iCloud, if you turned on iCloud backup), so we can't delete them for you, and we can't restore them after you delete them.

How do I back up to iCloud, or move to a new iPhone?

  • Turn it on: Settings › Backup & Data › Back up to iCloud. Sortd encrypts your purchases on your iPhone first, and keeps them up to date by itself. Tap Back Up Now to save straight away.
  • On a new iPhone: sign in to the same Apple Account with iCloud Keychain on, open Sortd, and tap Restore from iCloud. Choose Add What's Missing or Replace Everything.
  • Turn it off: switch it off and choose whether to delete the iCloud copy or keep it.

Do I need an account?

No. Every feature works without one. If you want, Settings › Account lets you sign in with Apple or Google. It helps us find you if you ask for help. Your purchases stay on your iPhone either way, and signing in with Google doesn't give Sortd your Gmail.

  • Sign out: Settings › Account › Sign Out. Your purchases stay.
  • Delete your account: Settings › Account › Delete Account. Sortd asks Apple or Google to cancel the sign-in and deletes your usage record. Choose Delete Account and All Data to wipe the app too.

How do I turn off usage data?

Settings › Privacy & Security › Privacy › Share usage data. Sortd counts which features get used and sends crash reports so we can fix them. It never sends amounts, shops, card digits or email. The one switch turns off both. See the privacy policy for exactly what's sent.

Is Sortd really free?

Yes. Every feature is free, for everyone. No subscription, no trial, no ads. If you paid for something in the app and want a refund, ask Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
