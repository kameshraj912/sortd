## How to test them
- Post one every 2–3 days, at the same time of day.
- **Hook test:** make Reel 1 twice, once with each opening line: "Watch me not type this in" and "Budgets die by February. Here's why." Keep everything else the same.
- After 72 hours, compare: 3-second hold rate, average watch time, shares, saves, profile visits and link taps. Don't compare likes.
- Whichever format wins (Show, PSA, trailer, talking head or POV), make three more in that style.
- Only boost a Reel that already did well without paying. No #ad needed on your own account, but creators you pay or gift must use it.
- **Hashtags:** 3 at most, e.g. #budgeting #iphoneapps #spendingtracker. Instagram's cap is 5, and fewer, more specific tags work better.
