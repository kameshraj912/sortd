Part 1 Get Sortd

  1. On your iPhone, open the TestFlight link from your Sortd email.
  2. If it asks, install TestFlight from the App Store. It’s free and made by Apple. Then open the link again.
  3. Tap Accept, then Install. Open Sortd and follow its screens:
When Sortd shows Log Apple Pay by itself, turn to Part 2A (iOS 27) or Part 2B (iOS 26).

Part 3

Check it works

✓

Pay with Apple Pay in a shop

Open Sortd and tap Activity. The purchase is already there. The ▶ button in Shortcuts doesn’t test this.

Example: S$5.20 at Kopitiam
✓

Sortd says it worked

⚙ › Purchase Sources › Apple Pay Logging shows Last tap logged.

Good to know

  • Apps and websites: on iOS 27 these log from Wallet’s notification. On iOS 26, add them with + in Sortd.
  • iOS 26, paid and nothing showed up: open the automation in Shortcuts. Amount, Shop and Card must each say Shortcut Input, and Run Immediately must be ticked.
  • Cash, or anything missed: tap + in Sortd.
  • Now and then Apple’s trigger misses a tap, mostly at vending machines, transport gates and parking.
  • Help: sortd.page/support
