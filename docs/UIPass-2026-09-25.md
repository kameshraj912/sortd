## Looked fine
- Continue-only setup reaches Home with no permission prompt, at default and at AX5.
- Swipe-delete, then Undo, brings the row back.
- Amount entry stops at 6 digits.
- Searching for spaces keeps the full list.
- Flipping months back and forth settles on the right totals.
- Home's last row clears the tab bar.
- Analytics off and on both save.
- Delete All Data returns to setup, clears the tip counters and keeps the analytics choice.
- The iCloud section is gone from Backup & Data.
- Dark mode is readable on every screen captured.

## Not checked
- Popover tips (swipe, search, month, insights): they skip sample rows, so demo data cannot trigger them. Not checked: one tip per screen, or tips covering the tab bar at AX5.
- Undo during the toast's fade: the toast could not be reproduced reliably (see the P1).
- The analytics switch: two plain taps did not flip it, a drag did. May be the tap tool, not the app.
- "+N converting", suggestion chips (need real history), Reduce Motion, the month slide direction, haptics, SE and Pro Max sizes, VoiceOver labels, tip purchase.
