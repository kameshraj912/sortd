StoreKit tip-jar tests that only fail on the simulator; no regressions), a whole-diff Opus
review, the abuse tester on the build 10 changes (bank-app notifications, three-way merges and

test in `SpendTests/AbuseBuild10Tests.swift`. "Traced" = read in the code or seen in a
screenshot, not run as a test.

How: a Wallet or bank refund now looks back 7 days for the same refund another trigger already
took off (tap-vs-tap keeps 10 minutes). A bank's refund needs a refund verb tied to the money
("Refund of", "refunded", "refund to your…"); a spend word before the amount and a refund verb
together save nothing. Equal amounts are counted before they are taken out. Every incoming
field is cut to 1,000 characters, and the "6.20 SGD" pattern starts only where a number starts.
