# Sortd brand voice

One page. Read it before writing anything public: a post, a caption, a reel, an App Store line or a reply.

## Who we sound like
A dry friend who's good with money and finds your habits a bit funny. Roasts like dbrand, breaks the fourth wall like Deadpool, and is cheap and fast like Maximum Effort's "fastvertising". It never shouts.

**Three words:** dry, honest, a bit smug.

## The one rule
**Roast the habit, the market or ourselves. Never the person.**
- ✅ "It was takeaway. It's always takeaway."
- ✅ "Most budgeting apps ask for your bank password. Weird ask."
- ✅ "This is our first post. They spent the whole budget on me. I'm AI."
- ❌ Anything about someone's looks, name, accent, background, income or job.

## The three jokes we tell
1. **Roast the habit.** Takeaway, "just one coffee", subscriptions you forgot about, spreadsheets that die by February.
2. **Roast the market.** Apps that want your bank login, a server, an account, your data. We want none of it.
3. **Roast ourselves.** Tiny budget, AI actor, beta bugs. Self-aware first, so the joke never feels like we're punching down.

## How the words work
- **Short sentences.** Plain words. Say the thing, then stop.
- **The fact first, the joke in brackets.** This is our house move:
  "Your data stays on your phone. (We don't even have a server to put it on.)"
- **One joke per line.** If a sentence needs two jokes, cut one.
- **A real number beats an adjective:** "$5.50 coffee", "row 4", "February".
- **Write the way people talk:** "anyway", "honestly", "weird ask".
- **Never use these:** game-changer, revolutionary, seamless, effortless, unlock, empower, "take control of your finances", exclamation marks, 🚀.

## Always true (check every claim)
Every joke has to rest on something true. Before posting, check that each claim still holds:
- No bank login. No account. No server. Data stays on the phone.
- Apple Pay taps are logged automatically after one Shortcuts setup.
- Free beta. The core app is free. (Pro exists, so don't call *everything* free.)
- Never show a fake app screen. Every app shot is a real recording of Sortd.
- AI actors are allowed, but never pass them off as real customers or real reviews. Joke about it instead: "I'm AI."

## Video rules (reels)
- **Hook in the first second, with text on screen**, so it works on mute.
- **One person, one place, one continuous take.** Hide the joins under app shots or cards. Never jump-cut a face.
- **App shots are real**, sit in a static phone frame (no zooms) and last 2–3 s.
- **Captions** show 1–3 words at a time, bold white with a thick black outline, and the current word in green `#3fd08f`. Keep them in the middle band of the screen, never in the bottom 25%.
- **Asides** go at the top in italics inside a dark pill, in brackets. One every ~8 s at most.
- **Length** 20–30 s. End on a deadpan beat, then the end card.
- **Brand colours:** orange `#f0643d`, amber `#f5a623`, violet `#7b6bf0`, green `#2bb07a`. Font: Inter Tight, weight 800.

## Calls to action
Pick one: "Free beta. Link in bio." · "Join the beta: sortd.page" · "Tap to pay. Sortd writes it down." Then close with the sign-off: "Consider it Sortd."
Never "Download now!!", "Don't miss out" or countdown pressure.

## The three lines (set 2 Oct 2026)
- **Headline, says what it does:** "Tap to pay. Sortd writes it down." (won the five-persona review.)
- **Sign-off:** "Consider it Sortd." End cards, the site footer, the last line of a post, the end of setup.
- **After a tap logs:** "You've been Sortd." Only where something has just happened: the first-purchase card, a reel's reveal.
- Always spelt **Sortd**, never "sorted". Never "Get Sortd": that is a registered Australian trade mark (see Brand/README.md).

## Quick test before posting
1. Would a friend actually say this out loud?
2. Is the joke on a habit, the market or us, and not on a person?
3. Is every claim true today?
4. Does it make sense on mute in the first second?
5. Could you cut a word? Then cut it.
