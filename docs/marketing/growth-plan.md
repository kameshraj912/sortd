# Growth plan — first 1,000 users

Written 21 Sep 2026 from a batch of app-founder reels. Two ideas worth keeping.

## 1. Strangers' money, not friends' praise

Friends and family always say they like it. That tells you nothing. The test is
whether a stranger pays.

- [ ] Most beta testers should come from sortd.page/beta, not people you know. Track
      the split.
- [ ] The beta gives Pro free (`SORTD_BETA`). Near the end of the beta, ask each
      tester one question: "Would you pay US$49.99 a year for this? Yes / No / Only if…"
      Count answers from strangers only.
- [ ] Offer testers a launch deal they have to act on: an App Store Offer Code for
      the first year at a discount, redeemable on launch day. Redemptions = real
      demand. (Offer Codes are single-use and Apple tracks them.)
- [ ] Target before launch: at least 20 strangers who said yes or redeemed.
      Below that, fix the product before spending on ads.

## 2. Organic posting at volume

One post a day doesn't break through. The Scratch golf app posted about 20 a day
early on; 5 a day is a realistic start for one person.

- [ ] **Format mix:** slideshows (fast to make, strong hooks) plus short reels from
      `claude-project/reels`. Slideshow hooks in the brand voice, e.g.
      "5 subscriptions you forgot you pay for", "Where $500 went last month (it was
      takeaway)".
- [ ] **Volume:** 3–5 posts a day across TikTok and Instagram for 30 days. Batch-make
      a week at a time.
- [ ] **Measure:** views per post, profile visits, beta sign-ups per day. Drop
      formats that stay under the median after 10 posts; make more of the top 20%.
- [ ] **Later:** paid ads (Apple Search Ads first, then Meta) only once organic shows
      which hooks convert. Optimise ads on a paid event (trial started), not installs.

Rules: follow `brand-voice.md`. Roast the habit, never the person. No fake reviews,
no bought followers.
