# Sortd cast bible

Three housemates in a Melbourne share house. Made in Google Flow (project "Sep 20 - 03:04",
Characters tab). Every reel starts from here so the guys never drift.

Rules for every script
- Jokes are about money habits, never about anyone's background, accent or looks.
- Nobody is dumb. Each guy is smart in his own way; the joke is the habit.
- Deadpan means flat punchlines with normal energy. Never low, tired or mumbly.
- They are AI characters. Never present them as real users or real reviews.
  Instagram and TikTok label AI people in ads; the scripts can own that as a joke.
- Any shot of the app is a real recording or real screenshot. Never generated.

## Mitch (lead)
- Look: white Australian, early 20s, messy sandy-brown hair, light stubble, freckles, slightly
  sunburnt nose. Grey crewneck t-shirt, small silver watch. Always this outfit.
- Voice: natural everyday Australian accent, not exaggerated. Confident, a slight smirk in his
  voice, flat on the punchline. Voice options: Mitch 1 / 2 / 3 (Aus).
- Who he is: the guy with the habits. Takeaway, subscriptions, "it's only $9". Knows it, owns it.
- Sample: "Groceries. ...Yeah, nah. It was takeaway."

## Nik (the mate filming)
- Look: South Asian, early 20s, short messy black hair, light stubble, faded navy hoodie.
- From Singapore, studying in Melbourne. Light, well-spoken Singaporean accent, standard
  English, no slang. Voice options: Nik 1 / 2 / 3 (light SG).
- Who he is: usually behind the phone. Cheeky, upbeat, asks the blunt question, laughs at Mitch.
  Pays in two currencies, so he's the one for SGD/AUD jokes.
- Sample: "Bro. Where'd your money go last month? Go on, guess."

## Ollie (the spreadsheet guy)
- Look: white, early 20s, curly ginger hair, thin wire glasses, freckles. Faded green flannel
  over a white t-shirt.
- English, on exchange from London. Well-spoken southern English accent. Precise and completely
  serious. Voice options: Ollie 1 / 2 / 3 (English).
- Who he is: over-organised. Built the colour-coded, forty-tab budget spreadsheet. Grieves it.
- Sample: "I made a spreadsheet. Colour-coded. Forty tabs. It died in February."

## Shared style block (end of every Veo prompt)
Casual handheld iPhone footage filmed by a housemate, vertical 9:16, natural light, slight
shake, imperfect framing, lived-in Melbourne share house or real café. Looks like a real
Instagram video, not a commercial, not cinematic. Natural conversational voices with breaths
and real timing, no announcer tone. No subtitles, no captions, no on-screen text, no logos or
brand names. Phone and laptop screens never face the camera.
