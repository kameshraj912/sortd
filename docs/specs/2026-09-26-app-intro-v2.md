# App intro v2: stories-style, auto-playing (26 Sep 2026)

Redo of `docs/specs/2026-09-25-app-intro.md` (approved 25 Sep, built as PR-worthy code
already in `Spend/Services/IntroTour.swift` and `Spend/Views/Components/IntroOverlay.swift`).
A mock exists; this doc describes it and plans the change. It does not redesign it.

**Approved by Raj on 26 Sep 2026: option A (stories). The mock is only a sketch of the behaviour; the real intro draws over the real screens.**

## Problem

"The click Next thing is wrong" (Raj): the built intro shows Next and Skip on every
step and waits for a tap before it does anything. Nothing plays on its own. Raj asked
for "little movements... within a couple of seconds" — motion that runs by itself,
Stories-style, not a slide deck you click through.

Reading the built code confirms two gaps, not one:
1. There is no auto-advance, no progress bar, and no pause-on-hold anywhere in
   `IntroOverlay.swift` — every step waits for **Next**.
2. Even the motion itself is thinner than the model implies. `IntroStep.motion(reduceMotion:)`
   returns `.tapTwice`, `.ringPulse` or `.swipe`, but `IntroOverlay.body`'s only animation is
   one shared ring-pulse (`pulse` @State, `.task(id: step)`) played identically for every
   step — there is no finger glyph anywhere in the file. So step 1 today does not actually
   show two taps; it shows the same ring as step 2. This rebuild needs real, distinct
   motion per step, not just new timing around the existing ring.

Also: sideways day-swipe is gone (PR #62, 26 Sep). Confirmed in `ActivityView.swift`:
`dayPager`'s only way to change day is the two chevron buttons calling `stepDay(_:)`; no
swipe gesture is bound to day paging. Step 3's line and target must follow that, not the
old "swipe to change the day."

## Behaviour, step by step

| Step | Target (cutout) | Motion | Line | Card position | Timing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Home | the + circle (`TabBarLayout.addCircle`, unchanged) | a finger taps it twice | "Tap + to add cash or anything Apple Pay missed" | just above the +, pointer toward it | motion plays ~1.5 s, holds to 2.5 s total, then auto-advances |
| 2 Insights | the Insights tab item; app really selects it | a ring pulses once around it | "Insights shows where your money goes" | above the tab bar | ring plays ~1.0 s, holds to 2.5 s, then auto-advances |
| 3 Activity | the day header row (chevrons + day title) — new cutout, not the whole bar | a finger taps the right ("Older Day") chevron once; `ActivityView.stepDay(1)` really runs if a second day exists, else only the tap plays | "Tap the arrows to move between days" | hangs under the header | tap plays ~1.0 s, holds to 2.5 s, then auto-advances (Done on step 3 ends the tour → Home) |

Every step: a tap anywhere advances early. A finger held down pauses the 2.5 s clock; lifting
resumes from where it paused, not from zero. Skip sits top right on every step, always. No
Next button while motion is allowed — Skip is the only button. Three thin progress segments
at the top of the screen (Stories-style) replace the card's old "Step 1 of 3" heading; the
card keeps a small "1 of 3" eyebrow underneath the progress bar, not as its own heading.

Start and end: begins 0.6 s after Home settles, and only once the setup sheet is fully gone
(i.e., after `RootView`'s existing eligibility check passes, not at the moment it first
becomes true — something else, like the add sheet, can still open in that 0.6 s window, so
the check must re-run after the delay, not just before it). Ends back on Home, same as today.

**Reduce Motion** (this is exactly what is built today, kept as the fallback): no finger, no
pulse, no auto-advance. A still ring. Next and Skip are real buttons. Steps cross-fade
(today's `.transition(.opacity)` + `.id(step)`), not the tap-anywhere Stories flow.

**VoiceOver**: modal (`.isModal`, unchanged), focus goes to the line first, nothing advances
on a timer, Next and Skip are real, reachable buttons — this must hold even when Reduce
Motion is off. Today's code only branches on `reduceMotion`; it needs a second gate so a
VoiceOver user never lands in the tap-anywhere/auto-advance mode. **Not verified**: the exact
environment key (`accessibilityVoiceOverEnabled`) compiles as expected in this codebase —
not seen compile here.

Works with no purchases (step 3's "else only the tap plays" case covers a fresh, empty
Activity). Existing installs that already saw the old intro do not see this one automatically
— only Help › Show the Intro Again (`intro.replay()`, unchanged) replays it, per the standing
rule that seen stays seen.

## Timer: two options

**A. Task-based per-step timer, pause via long press (recommended)**
- The pure model gets a small value type (in `IntroTour.swift`, alongside `IntroStep`), e.g.
  `IntroCountdown { var stepStartedAt: Date; var pausedAt: Date?; var pausedTotal: TimeInterval }`,
  with `remaining(at:duration:) -> TimeInterval`, `pause(at:)`, `resume(at:)`. Pure Date math,
  no sleeping — testable with injected `Date`s, no waiting in tests.
- The view drives it: extend the existing `.task(id: step)` in `IntroOverlay` to also poll
  every ~100 ms and call `onNext()` once `remaining <= 0`; `.onLongPressGesture(minimumDuration: 0.01,
  pressing: { down in ... call pause/resume on the countdown ... })` on the dim layer, next to
  the existing `.onTapGesture { onNext() }`.
- Cost: ~0.5 d beyond the model; reuses the file's existing per-step `Task` and gesture pattern.
- Risk: 100 ms poll granularity is coarse enough it can't drift the 2.5 s budget noticeably,
  but it is still a polling loop, not an exact deadline.
- **Not verified**: `.onLongPressGesture(minimumDuration:pressing:)` compiling in this file
  as written — a stable, long-standing SwiftUI modifier, but not seen compile here.

**B. `TimelineView`/Timer publisher**
- `TimelineView(.animation(minimumInterval: 0.1, paused: isPressing))` (the same shape
  `SetupPages.swift:14` already uses for a different animation) wraps the countdown read, or a
  Combine `Timer.publish(...).autoconnect()` subscribed via `.onReceive`.
- `paused:` gives pause-on-hold for free, which is attractive, but the whole `IntroOverlay`
  body (dim, cutout, ring, card) would re-render on every tick if the `TimelineView` wraps it
  broadly; scoping it to just the countdown read avoids that but adds a second timing
  mechanism next to the existing `.task`-based motion animation — two clocks driving one card.
- No Combine import exists anywhere in `Spend/` today; introducing one for this alone is new
  surface for a small gain.

**Recommendation: A.** It stays in the same structured-concurrency style the file already
uses for the motion animation (one `.task(id: step)`, cancelled for free on step change), adds
no new import, and keeps "pause resumes remaining time" as pure, fast-testable Date math
rather than something that has to be proven against a live `TimelineView` tick. First step:
extend `IntroCountdown` and its tests before touching the View.

## What it touches

- **`Spend/Services/IntroTour.swift`**
  - `IntroStep.motion(reduceMotion:)` — keep, but motion cases likely need a rename/split so
    the View can actually draw them apart (see Problem §2): e.g. `.tapTwice` (step 1),
    `.ringPulse` (step 2, unchanged), a new single-tap case for step 3 (today's `.swipe` no
    longer describes anything real).
  - Add `IntroCountdown` (Option A, above): pure, `Date`-driven, own tests.
  - `IntroTour` gains an "was every advance a timeout, not a tap" accumulator (e.g.
    `private(set) var autoOnly = true`, set false by whichever call means "the user acted early"),
    read out at `finish(skipped:)` as the `auto` flag for Analytics. `skip()` always yields
    `auto == false`.
  - Copy: `IntroStep.line` for `.move` changes to "Tap the arrows to move between days"; drop
    "Switch tabs here." `voiceOverTarget` for `.move` follows.
- **`Spend/Views/Components/IntroOverlay.swift`**
  - New: the top progress bar (three thin segments, Stories-style), replacing the card's old
    "Step N of 3" text with a smaller "N of 3" eyebrow under the bar.
  - New: per-motion view builders that actually draw a finger (double-tap for step 1,
    single-tap for step 3) next to the kept ring-pulse for step 2 — today's shared `pulse`
    animation is not enough on its own (Problem §2).
  - New: no Next button while motion is allowed (`!reduceMotion && !voiceOverRunning`); Skip
    only. Long-press-to-pause gesture alongside the existing tap-to-advance one.
  - `targetFrame(for:probe:screen:safeBottom:)` needs a fourth input for step `.move`: the day
    header's frame, which is a plain SwiftUI view inside `ActivityView`'s `List`, not a UIKit
    control `TabBarProbe` can find by class name. Proposed: a `PreferenceKey` (e.g.
    `IntroDayHeaderKey`, `CGRect?`) set on `ActivityView.pagerHeader(_:position:)`, read where
    `IntroOverlay` is placed via `.overlayPreferenceValue(_:_:)`, and threaded into
    `IntroOverlay` as a new parameter. **Spike this first, half a day**: `List` inside
    `ScrollViewReader` has, in the past, interfered with preference propagation to an ancestor;
    **not verified** whether it does here. Fallback if it does: keep the whole-bar cutout for
    step 3, as today.
- **`Spend/App/SpendApp.swift` (`RootView`)**
  - `startIntroIfEligible()`: wrap the eligibility check in a 0.6 s delay (`Task { try? await
    Task.sleep(for: .milliseconds(600)); ... re-check the same guards ... }`), instead of
    calling `intro.start()` immediately — the guards must be re-read after the sleep, since a
    sheet can open during it.
  - Add a `Router` flag (mirroring `pendingIntroReplay`, e.g. `pendingIntroDayTap`) that
    `RootView` sets when the `.move` step's motion starts, and `ActivityView` observes and
    clears; only `ActivityView` knows whether a second day exists, so the "call `stepDay(1)`
    if a second day exists" check lives there, not in `RootView` or `IntroOverlay`.
  - The `.onChange(of: intro, initial: true)` block already sends `introShown`/`introFinished`
    with `skipped`/`step`; add `"auto": .bool(new.autoOnly-at-finish)` to the `introFinished`
    payload.
- **`Spend/Views/ActivityView.swift`**
  - `pagerHeader(_:position:)`: report its frame for the preference key above (only while the
    intro is actually on step `.move`, so this costs nothing the rest of the time).
  - New `.onChange(of: router.pendingIntroDayTap)` (or similar): if `days.count > 1`, call the
    existing private `stepDay(1)`; either way, clear the flag. `stepDay(_ delta: Int)` itself
    is unchanged.
- **`Spend/Services/Analytics.swift`**
  - `Event.introFinished` payload gains `auto: Bool`. No new event names.

No SwiftData model change, so no migration plan and nothing for `MigrationTests`.
No pbxproj edits (folder-synced groups).

## Risks

- **The day-header cutout can't be measured.** `ui-driver` and a manual pass on iOS 26 and 27
  would show a blank/whole-bar cutout instead of the header. Fallback: keep step 3's cutout on
  the whole tab bar, as the currently-built version does, and only change the line and the tap
  target's real action.
- **VoiceOver falls into the auto-advance path by mistake** if the new gate only checks
  `reduceMotion` (an easy copy-paste of the existing pattern). A VoiceOver user would get a
  timer they can't stop. Checked with the accessibility tree, VoiceOver on, Reduce Motion off.
- **The 0.6 s start delay races something else opening** (the add sheet, Settings, App Lock).
  If the re-check after the sleep is missed, the intro could start over another sheet. Checked
  by `ui-driver` opening the add sheet in that window.
- **Privacy label / App Review**: none new — `auto` is a boolean on an event already sent
  (usage data, not content). Data: none. This assumes the label already declares usage data,
  same assumption as the 25 Sep spec, **not verified** here either.
- **Data loss**: none — no model touched.

## Test plan (`SpendTests`, mostly `IntroTourTests` plus one new suite for `IntroCountdown`)

Pure model:
- Step order unchanged: `IntroTour.steps == [.add, .insights, .move]`.
- `.move`'s line is "Tap the arrows to move between days"; does not mention "swipe."
- `IntroCountdown.remaining(at:duration:)` counts down correctly from `stepStartedAt`.
- `pause(at:)` then `resume(at:)` later preserves the remaining time (does not restart at
  `duration`, does not keep counting through the paused gap).
- A tap (`onNext()`/model `next()`) advances immediately regardless of remaining time.
- `everyStepHasNoMotionUnderReduceMotion` (existing test): still true — Reduce Motion still
  turns motion off; add a matching one for the timer: under Reduce Motion the countdown is
  never consulted / auto-advance never fires (however the code ends up gating this — the test
  should assert the observable outcome, not an implementation detail).
- `auto` flag: three untouched steps in a row (all timeouts) → `auto == true` at finish; one
  early tap anywhere in the run → `auto == false` at finish; `skip()` → `auto == false` always.
- Existing tests to keep green or update: `stepMotionDurationsSumUnderNineSeconds`,
  `linesAreShortAndPlain`, `voiceOverLabelsNameTheirControl` (the `.move` label's wording
  changes, so its string check needs to follow the new line), `voiceOverLabelsCarryTheStepPosition`.

`ui-driver` (fresh simulator, tap through setup):
- The three progress segments show at the top, one fills per step.
- The card shows "1 of 3" (not "Step 1 of 3") once the segments are on screen.
- Tapping anywhere advances early; the segment jumps to the next one.
- Holding a finger down pauses the segment fill; lifting resumes it (not restarts it).
- Skip is reachable on every step; no Next button appears while motion is allowed.
- The day-header cutout sits on the header row on Activity, not the whole bar (or the
  documented fallback, if the spike says the header can't be measured).
- With a second day of demo data present, the right chevron tap really changes the day.
- With only one day of data, the tap motion still plays and nothing errors.
- Reduce Motion: no finger, no pulse, no auto-advance, Next/Skip visible, cross-fade between
  steps.
- VoiceOver: the line is read first, nothing times out, Next and Skip are both reachable.
- Dark mode; SE, 16 Pro, 16 Pro Max; iOS 26 and 27.

**Device (Raj)**: feel of the auto-advance pace (2.5 s, does it feel rushed or slow), whether
holding to pause is discoverable without being told, and the real day change on Activity.

## Open questions

1. Is "auto" per-finish (did any step get tapped early) the right shape, or should it instead
   count how many of the three steps were auto-advanced (an int, not a bool)?
2. If the day-header spike fails, is the whole-bar fallback for step 3 acceptable, or does
   that step wait until the measurement works?
3. Exact wording check: "Tap the arrows to move between days" — say "arrows" or "chevrons"?
   (Chevrons is the accessibility label already in code; arrows reads plainer.)
