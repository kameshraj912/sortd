# App intro: three quick steps after setup (25 Sep 2026)

**Approved by Raj on 25 Sep 2026 with the defaults:** remove the test tap outright; Finish Setup ticks Apple Pay when the Shortcut first reaches the app; intro step 3 shows the tab bar then a swipe between days; existing installs see the intro once; keep five tips and drop the add tip.

## Problem

Raj on his iPhone 16 Pro Max: "The tips thing isn't what I meant. After onboarding, a small three-slide thing: this is where you press to add the card, this is where you view insights, this is how you move through the app. Little movements that show where to click, within a couple of seconds."

Today, when setup closes, you land on Home with nothing pointing anywhere. The six TipKit tips (`Components/Tips.swift`, sub-spec 7) never show in the first session, and after that only one a day. So a new user gets no guidance in the first few minutes.

## The intro

The intro shows once, right after setup's `finish()` (`RootView` sees `setupPresented` turn false). It can be replayed from Help › Show the Intro Again.

| Step | App goes to | Highlighted | Line (draft) | Motion |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 add | Home | the + circle | "Tap + to add cash or anything Apple Pay missed." | a finger taps the + twice |
| 2 insights | Insights (really selected) | the tab bar, ring on Insights | "Insights shows where your money goes." | a ring pulses once |
| 3 move | Activity | the whole tab bar | "Tap a tab to move around. On Activity, swipe to change day." | a hand swipes sideways once |

How it behaves:
- **Look.** The screen dims to about 55% black, with a cutout over the target. A small glass card carries the line, "1 of 3", **Next** (Done on step 3) and **Skip**. Tapping anywhere advances.
- **Timing.** Each animation runs once, in 1.5 s or less, then holds. Next works at once. Going straight through takes under 10 s.
- **Reduce Motion.** No moving finger. A still ring and an arrow, and steps cross-fade. Use `accessibilityReduceMotion` only; `accessibilityPrefersCrossFadeTransitions` is SDK-only (HANDOVER trap).
- **VoiceOver.** The overlay is modal (`.isModal`). Focus goes to the line, read as "Step 1 of 3. The plus button, bottom right, adds a purchase." Nothing behind it can be reached. Nothing advances on a timer.
- **Where it ends.** Done returns to Home. Skip counts as seen, per HIG: don't show it again, keep it easy to find.
- **Empty app.** It works with no purchases, which is the normal case after setup. Step 3 does not need any days to exist.

## Options

| | A. SwiftUI overlay, measured cutouts (recommended) | B. TipKit popovers, chained | C. 3-page sheet with pictures |
|---|---|---|---|
| Matches Raj's ask (real screen, dim, motion) | yes | partly: no dim, no motion | no, it shows pictures of the app |
| HIG | "teach through interactivity", optional, brief | "context-specific tips" | an optional tutorial is allowed, but NN/g found card decks don't help |
| Work | 2–2.5 d | 1 d | 1 d |
| At AX5 | our card; it scrolls and sits above the bar | popovers covered the tab bar before (UI pass) | best |
| Main risk | the tab bar is drawn by the system (below) | `.displayFrequency(.daily)` blocks a chain unless every tip sets `IgnoresDisplayFrequency`, which also changes how the other tips are counted | ignored |

**The trap for A and B: the + and Insights are `Tab` items in a system tab bar** (`RootView.tabs`, `NavLayout.prominent`). I expect a `Tab` label to take no `anchorPreference` or `.popoverTip` (**not verified**). So the cutout comes from the tab bar area instead, measured with `onGeometryChange` from a tab's bottom `safeAreaInsets` (**not verified** on iOS 26 glass bars).
- The + is the separate circle at the trailing end. The code says this is true on both iOS 26 and 27.
- On step 2 the app really selects Insights, so the system draws the highlight on the right item.
- `anchorPreference` is kept for our own views, such as `AddFAB` in the `.fab` layout.

## Recommendation

**A.** It is the only option that shows the real control moving, which is what Raj asked for. Selecting the tab for real keeps it correct even if our measurement of the bar is slightly off. First step: a 1-hour spike on the iOS 26 and 27 simulators to measure the bar area and the + circle. If it misses, fall back to a glow over the whole bar area with an arrow. Then build the pure `IntroTour` model and its tests.

**Tips: drop only `AddTip` and keep the other five.** The intro now teaches +. The other five teach things the intro does not: row swipe actions, search, period chips, the month menu and Apple Pay setup. They are built, tested, one a day, and never shown in the first session. Also add `TipState.introShowing`, which blocks tips the way `setupShowing` does.

## Risks

- **The cutout misses the control on one iOS version, or with the bar minimised.** `ui-driver` screenshots on both simulators would show it. The fallback is the bar-wide glow.
- **The intro fights other screens:** setup closing (the 0.6 s double-tap guard), App Lock, the ActivationCard, the add sheet. The rule is to show it only when nothing else is up. `ui-driver` checks each case.
- **A VoiceOver user gets trapped.** Skip must always be reachable. Checked with the accessibility tree.
- **Privacy label:** two new analytics events of a type we already send (usage). This assumes the label already declares usage data (**not verified**). App Review: none. Data: none.

## Test plan (Swift Testing, `IntroTourTests`)

- `steps == [.add, .insights, .move]`.
- `start()` → step 0.
- `next()` twice → `.move`. `next()` on the last step → `finished`, `seen == true`, `skipped == false`.
- `skip()` at any step → `finished`, `seen == true`, `skipped == true`.
- `shouldShow`:
  - `(setupDone: true, seen: false, setupShowing: false, locked: false)` → true;
  - any one of seen, setupShowing, locked or `!setupDone` → false.
- `replay()` → `shouldShow` true even when seen. After it finishes → seen still true.
- Tab for each step: `.add → .home`, `.insights → .insights`, `.move → .activity`. After finishing → `.home`.
- Sum of step animation times ≤ 9 s. With Reduce Motion on, every step's motion is `.none`.
- Copy: each line ≤ 60 characters, has no "Pro", and each VoiceOver label names the control.
- While the intro is showing, `TipState.compute()` returns every tip false.
- `TipCopy.all.count == 5`. Update `TipRulesTests` (lines 13–21, 107), `TipStateTests:50` and `TipCopyTests` for the removed add tip.
- `intro_shown` is sent once. `intro_finished` carries `skipped` and `step`.

**ui-driver** (fresh simulator; tap Continue through setup):
- The intro appears within 1 s on Home.
- The cutout sits on the + circle, Insights and the bar, on SE, 16 Pro and 16 Pro Max, iOS 26 and 27.
- At AX5 the card does not cover the target.
- With Reduce Motion, nothing moves.
- With VoiceOver, the step is read first, Skip and Next are reachable, and nothing behind them is.
- Dark mode.
- Help › Show the Intro Again replays it.
- The tabs behind really switch.

**Device (Raj, 16 Pro Max):** feel and speed; the whole thing takes under 10 s.
